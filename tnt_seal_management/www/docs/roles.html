Customer Portal User

Manual for external clients submitting seal requests and monitoring active shipments.

Portal Onboarding

Clients gain access to the Customer Portal (/customer-portal) via invitation emails sent by system managers. Your login must be linked to exactly one Customer account. The portal has four tabs:

  • New Tagging Booking: request a seal tagging service.
  • My Tagging Bookings: track your bookings (Submitted for Review → Under Internal Review → Confirmed / Not Approved) and cancel one that is still Submitted for Review.
  • Completed Journeys: a statement of your finished journeys and their charges for a chosen period (Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Custom), exportable to PDF or Excel.
  • Sales Orders: the orders raised for your completed journeys, which you Accept or Reject.

Tutorial 1: Requesting a Tagging

  1. Open the form: Go to the New Tagging Booking tab.
  2. Fill in the details: Enter the Location, the Requested Date and Time (must be in the future), and the on-site contact's Name and Phone Number.
  3. Submit: Click Submit Tagging Booking. Your Account Manager is notified and reviews it.
  4. Track it: Follow its status on My Tagging Bookings. Use Cancel there if you no longer need it (only while Submitted for Review).

Tutorial 2: Responding to a Sales Order

When your completed journeys are billed, a Sales Order is raised and you are emailed:

  1. Open it: Go to the Sales Orders tab and click View on the order to see the journeys and charges it covers.
  2. Respond: Click Accept, or Reject with a reason (required). Your response is final and Finance is notified.

You are notified when…

You receive an email for each of these (portal users have no Desk bell).

  • A Sales Order is raised for your completed journeys and is waiting for your response

Account Manager

Raising tagging bookings for clients, reviewing customer portal bookings, and submitting them to Finance.

Tutorial: Reviewing and Submitting a Booking

  1. Open the booking: Use the bell notification link or the Tagging Booking List. Portal bookings arrive as Pending Account Manager Review; bookings you raise yourself start as Draft.
  2. Claim it (shared queue only): If the portal booking has no Account Manager yet, click Assign to Me.
  3. Check the details: Select the Branch (required; portal bookings arrive without one), make sure the date and time are not in the past, and that every vehicle is registered to the client.
  4. Submit: Click Submit to Finance. Every Finance PCB user is notified to approve it.
  5. If Finance sends it back: you are notified with their remarks. Correct the booking and click Submit to Finance again.

You are notified when…

Each notification arrives in the Desk bell icon (click it to open the record) and by email.

  • A customer books on the portal and it is assigned to you (or lands in the shared queue)
  • Finance returns a booking for amendment, reopens an approved booking, or rejects it

PCB Team Leader

Supervising field technicians and dispatching operators for tagging, untagging, and seal return assignments.

Field Dispatch Workflows

The PCB Team Leader acts as the bridge between Operations and the Field Tagging Agents. They receive auto-generated PCB Assignments when journeys start or end, and are responsible for assigning a specific Tag Operator to complete the physical task.

Tutorial 1: Dispatching for Tagging (Start of Journey)

When Finance approves a tagging booking they choose you as its Team Leader; a tagging request appears on your Assignments page and you are notified:

  1. Open Assignment: Use the bell notification link, or open Assignments and find the new Pending tagging request.
  2. Select Operator: In the assignment form, select the target Assigned Field Technician from your team.
  3. Dispatch: Click Actions > Assign. One Journey Request is created covering every vehicle on the booking, each vehicle's journey moves to Technician Assigned, and the technician is notified.

Tutorial 2: Dispatching for Untagging / Seal Return (End of Journey)

When the Control Room triggers a physical or remote unlock at the destination, an Untagging or Seal Return assignment is raised:

  1. Open Assignment: Access the newly generated Untagging or Seal Return assignment.
  2. Select Operator: Pick the Assigned Field Technician who will physically retrieve or cut the seal.
  3. Save (Auto-Dispatch): Unlike Tagging, there is no separate "Assign" button for end-of-journey tasks. Simply clicking Save on the document auto-progresses the assignment and instantly hands the task to the field agent, who is notified.

You are notified when…

Each notification arrives in the Desk bell icon (click it to open the record) and by email.

  • Finance approves a booking and chooses you as its Team Leader, or hands an existing request over to you
  • The Control Room confirms arrival with a physical or remote unlock (new Untagging / Seal Return assignment)
  • A Field Technician confirms a seal return and it needs your approval

Field Tagging Agent

Complete guides for physical lock installation, checklists execution, and border/port untagging operations.

Technician Responsibilities

Field agents are localized at ports and borders. They receive assignments from Team Leaders and physically manage the seal hardware. Below are the three core workflows for the field.

Tutorial 1: Journey Tagging (Start of Transit)

When you are assigned a tagging job you are notified, and the Journey Request waits for you in Draft. One request covers every vehicle on the booking:

  1. Open Request: Use the bell notification link or open the assigned Journey Request.
  2. Fill in the details: File number, departure card number, route and the Seals. With two or more vehicles, set the Vehicle column on each seal row to the vehicle it is fitted to (it is pre-filled; every vehicle needs at least one seal).
  3. Pre-Tagging Checks: Complete every item on the Pre-Tagging Checklist and upload at least one Tagging Picture.
  4. Submit: Click Submit to Control Room (on the Seals table). With several vehicles, untick any you are not ready to submit; they stay in Draft for later.
  5. Wait for approval: You are notified when the Control Room approves a vehicle, or returns it with remarks to correct and resubmit.
  6. Complete Tagging: Fit the seals and click Complete Tagging. With several approved vehicles, pick one or all. The seal's live GPS location is recorded and that vehicle's journey goes In Transit. This cannot be undone.

Tutorial 2: Journey Untagging (End of Transit)

After a physical unlock at the destination, the PCB Team Leader assigns you the untagging job (you are notified) and that vehicle on the Journey Request moves to Untagging:

  1. Open Request: Use the bell notification link or open the assigned Journey Request.
  2. Physical Removal: Physically remove the seal from the container or truck.
  3. Capture Evidence (Optional): Upload photos under Untagging Documents > Entry Pictures.
  4. Confirm: Click Confirm Untagging (with several vehicles, pick one or all) and add any remarks in the dialog. The location is captured live from the seal's GPS; if that is unavailable you are asked to type it. The vehicle moves to Awaiting Seal Return and the seal stays in your custody.

Tutorial 3: Seal Return (Custody Handoff)

After untagging (or a remote unlock, where the Team Leader assigns you a Seal Return job), the vehicle is in Awaiting Seal Return:

  1. Transport: Take the seal hardware back to the warehouse.
  2. Capture Evidence (Optional): Upload photos under Seal Return Documents & Photos > Entry Pictures (Seal Return).
  3. Confirm: Click Confirm Seal Return (with several vehicles, pick one or all). In the dialog tick that the seal was physically returned and enter the Seal Condition (Good, Damaged or Lost), Retrieval Card Number and Warehouse (all required), plus optional remarks. The location is captured from the seal's GPS (or typed if unavailable).
  4. Approval: The PCB Team Leader is notified to approve the return. You are notified when they approve it (journey Completed, seal back in the warehouse) or send it back for amendment.

You are notified when…

Each notification arrives in the Desk bell icon (click it to open the record) and by email.

  • You are assigned a Tagging, Untagging or Seal Return job
  • The Control Room approves a vehicle (complete tagging) or returns it for amendment
  • The PCB Team Leader approves your seal return or returns it for amendment
  • Your assignment is cancelled

Control Room Operator

Protocol for monitoring transits, approving journey setups, resolving exceptions, and handling tampering alarms.

Operations Room Workflows

Operators serve as the central nervous system for all transits, keeping constant track of active journeys via the Control Room Dashboard and acting as the final gatekeeper before a journey commences.

Tutorial 1: Journey Setup & Approvals

Before a container begins its transit, you must approve the hardware assignment. You are notified whenever a technician submits a request:

  1. View Pending Requests: Open the Control Room page, Approve tab. Each request waiting for you is a card (it stays there while any of its vehicles is pending).
  2. Hardware Check: Click Refresh Seal Status to pull live lock, battery and location. On a multi-vehicle request the seal table's Vehicle column shows which vehicle each seal is fitted to. To replace a defective seal, open the Journey Request and use Seals > Swap Seal.
  3. Approve or return each vehicle: The card's Vehicles table has Approve (remarks optional) and Return (remarks required) per pending vehicle. Use Approve All Pending / Return All Pending to act on every pending vehicle at once (on a single-vehicle request these read Approve / Return for Amendment).
  4. Result: An approved vehicle moves to Tagging and the technician is notified to complete tagging; a returned vehicle goes back to Draft and the technician is notified with your remarks.

Tutorial 2: Alert Monitoring & Resolution

When a seal reports an anomaly (e.g. tamper, low battery, lost connection) an alert is raised. Critical alerts are also sent to you as a notification:

  1. Monitor Alerts: Open the Control Room page and switch to the Alert tab. It lists only alerts for seals on journeys that are In Transit (alerts on seals in stock, awaiting return or on finished journeys are left out). Filter by status (Open, Resolved, All), level and type; click an alert's status to see its full details.
  2. Action: Click Action on the alert row (Update if it is already Escalated).
  3. Resolve: Choose Resolved (handled or a false alarm), log what was done in Remarks and click Submit. The alert closes.
  4. Escalate with an email: Choose Escalated (acknowledged, more action needed). An Escalation Email section appears, pre-filled with the alert details and addressed to whoever holds the seal (when they have an email on file). Change or add recipients (comma-separated), add CC, edit the subject and message, then click Submit. The alert is escalated and the email sent in one step; it is recorded on the alert's timeline and noted in its remarks. Untick Send an email with this escalation to escalate without emailing.

Tutorial: Unlocking a Seal (End Journey)

When a container reaches its destination, confirm arrival and how the seal was unlocked:

  1. Open Control Room: Go to the Control Room page, Approve tab, and scroll to Arrivals — Confirm Seal Unlocked (every journey In Transit; use the search box to filter).
  2. Select Journey: Click View on the journey. Each vehicle of a multi-vehicle booking has its own journey.
  3. Select Mode:
    • Physical Unlock — Send for Untagging: creates an untagging assignment; the PCB Team Leader is notified.
    • Remote Unlock — Skip to Seal Return: skips untagging and creates a seal return assignment; the PCB Team Leader is notified.
    • Remote Unlock — Seal Stays on Vehicle: destination too far to collect from; the journey completes immediately and the seal is released.
    • End Journey: nobody at the destination; same outcome as the option above.
  4. Confirm: Accept the prompt. The arrival location is captured live from the seal's GPS. This cannot be undone.

You are notified when…

Each notification arrives in the Desk bell icon (click it to open the record) and by email.

  • A Field Technician submits a Journey Request for approval
  • A critical seal alert is raised for a journey in transit

Finance Manager

Managing client contracts, seal billing rates, approving customer bookings, and reviewing/settling monthly statements.

Invoicing and Approvals

Ensuring all tracking operations are billed accurately according to contracts. The Finance Manager is responsible for authorizing jobs before field agents are deployed, setting up rate rules, and settling finalized trips.

Tutorial 1: Booking Approvals (Job Order Creation)

Before a tagging job can start, Finance must approve the customer booking:

  1. View Bookings: You are notified when a booking is submitted; use the bell link or open the Tagging Booking List and filter for requests in the Pending Finance PCB Approval status.
  2. Review Contract: Open the booking and ensure the customer's financial account is in good standing.
  3. Approve or Amend:
    • Click Actions > Approve and choose the PCB Team Leader who will dispatch it (pre-filled when there is only one). This creates the PCB Job Order and sends the tagging request straight to that Team Leader, who is notified. To hand it to someone else before a Tag Operator is assigned, open the assignment from Assignments and use Actions > Change Team Leader.
    • If a booking still pending approval needs correction, click Actions > Amend; for an already approved booking use Actions > Reopen / Amend. Give a reason; the Account Manager is notified with it.

Tutorial 2: How to Set Billing for a Customer

Finance Managers apply custom rules to clients via the Current Customers module:

  1. Navigate: Open the Current Customers page.
  2. Open Modal: Find the target customer and click Set billing (or the existing rule label) in the Billing Type column.
  3. Choose Type: Select either Subscription or Leasing.
    • If Subscription: Pick a pre-configured Billing Rule from the dropdown. If the customer purchased their devices, check Outright Purchase and enter the Number of Seals Owned.
    • If Leasing: Proceed to the Rate Terms section to manually input the Currency, First Period Amount, First Period Days, and Extra Day Rate.
  4. Save: Verify the Tax category and click Save Billing to apply the rules. A new or changed billing rule goes to the Managing Director for approval (they are notified), and the customer stays disabled until it is approved.

Tutorial 3: Invoicing Completed Journeys

Completed journeys are billed per customer in batches from the Completed Journeys page:

  1. Choose the period: Open Completed Journeys and set the period / From and To dates. Each customer's unbilled completed journeys are grouped with their normal charges, extra-day charges, subscription fees and VAT.
  2. Review: Check the charges summary for the customer. Export to PDF or Excel if needed.
  3. Generate: Click Generate Sales Order and confirm. The journeys move to Processing Payment and the customer is emailed to Accept or Reject the order on the portal.
  4. Customer response: You are notified when the customer accepts or rejects (with their reason). Follow up on rejections.
  5. Settlement: Once the Sales Invoice for the order is fully paid, its journeys are marked Billed automatically.

You are notified when…

Each notification arrives in the Desk bell icon (click it to open the record) and by email.

  • An Account Manager submits a booking for your approval
  • A PCB Assignment is cancelled and its journey reverts to Pending Finance PCB Approval
  • A customer accepts or rejects a Sales Order on the portal
  • The Managing Director approves or rejects a billing rule you created

Managing Director

Signing off on billing rules before they can be used to charge customers.

Tutorial: Approving a Billing Rule

Every new billing rule, and any approved rule whose terms are changed, waits for your approval. Customers on it stay disabled until you approve:

  1. Open the rule: Use the bell notification link, or open Seal Billing Rates and look for Pending Approval.
  2. Review the terms: Check the billing type, period, first-period amount and days, and extra-day rate.
  3. Decide: Click Approve, or Reject with remarks. Whoever created the rule is notified.

You are notified when…

Each notification arrives in the Desk bell icon (click it to open the record) and by email.

  • A new billing rule, or a change to an approved one, needs your approval

System Administrator

Configuring API keys, user management, and setting up master checkpoints.

App Configuration

Maintains the foundational backend of the custom app:

  • Seal API Settings: the telemetry API connection (base URL, project, authentication), Sync Frequency Minutes, the optional Full Fleet Sync and its interval, request throttling, and the Pre-Tagging Checklist Template copied into every new Journey Request. Last sync times and errors are shown here.
  • Notifications: workflow notifications are emailed through the site's default outgoing Email Account, so keep it enabled and working. Every user must have an email address and the right role (Account Manager, Finance PCB, PCB Team Leader, Field Technician, Operations Control Room, Managing Director) to be notified. See the Notifications section of the Features guide for the full list.
  • Master Data: manage custody points (warehouses), transport locations (checkpoints), vehicles and their owning customers, and seal devices.
