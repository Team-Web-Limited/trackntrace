Customer Portal User

Manual for external clients submitting seal requests and monitoring active shipments.

Portal Onboarding

Clients gain access to the Customer Portal via invitation emails sent by system managers. Once authenticated, the portal provides a self-service tagging booking interface.

  • Booking Requests: Submit origin locations, destinations, scheduled date/times, and required quantities of e-seals.
  • Tracking Logs: View history logs of active and completed journey requests associated with the client's corporate account.

Tutorial: Viewing Journeys

To view active shipments, navigate to the Journeys tab on your portal. You can view real-time location data on the integrated map and check the status (e.g., Tagged, Unlocked) of each container.

Customer Care Agent

Guide for Customer Care representatives managing client accounts, onboarding external portal users, and coordinating transit dispatches.

1. Customer Onboarding & Support

Customer Care serves as the primary liaison between external shipping clients and the logistics team. Core responsibilities include:

  • Client Onboarding: Verify client details, configure portal credentials, and issue invitations for Customer Portal access.
  • Booking Assistance: Assist customers with submitting booking requests, updating transit information, and responding to scheduling changes.

2. Journey Setup & Coordination

Customer Care coordinates dispatch details to ensure they match active transport operations:

  • Reviewing Bookings: Cross-check client requests for container details, transport routes, and contact numbers.
  • Pre-Transit Approvals: Once details are verified, register your user approval in the Customer Care Section. Fill in the Customer Care Remarks to record any deviations or special instructions, and save to stamp your name and approval time.

Tutorial: Handling Escalations

When a customer portal user reports an issue, navigate to the customer's Journey Request. Review their submitted details. Use the Customer Care Remarks field to leave notes for the Control Room or Finance teams if the request requires adjustments before approval.

PCB Team Leader

Supervising field technicians and dispatching operators for tagging, untagging, and seal return assignments.

Field Dispatch Workflows

The PCB Team Leader acts as the bridge between Operations and the Field Tagging Agents. They receive auto-generated PCB Assignments when journeys start or end, and are responsible for assigning a specific Tag Operator to complete the physical task.

Tutorial 1: Dispatching for Tagging (Start of Journey)

When Finance approves a tagging booking, a Job Order is created and a Tagging Assignment is routed to you:

  1. Open Assignment: Open the PCB Assignment List and find the new assignment (or open it via the PCB Job Order).
  2. Select Operator: In the assignment form, select the target Assigned Field Technician from your team.
  3. Dispatch: Click Actions > Assign. This will formally pass the job to the selected agent's queue and update the overarching journey status to Tagging.

Tutorial 2: Dispatching for Untagging / Seal Return (End of Journey)

When the Control Room triggers a physical or remote unlock at the destination, an Untagging or Seal Return assignment is raised:

  1. Open Assignment: Access the newly generated Untagging or Seal Return assignment.
  2. Select Operator: Pick the Assigned Field Technician who will physically retrieve or cut the seal.
  3. Save (Auto-Dispatch): Unlike Tagging, there is no separate "Assign" button for end-of-journey tasks. Simply clicking Save on the document auto-progresses the assignment and instantly hands the task to the field agent.

Field Tagging Agent

Complete guides for physical lock installation, checklists execution, and border/port untagging operations.

Technician Responsibilities

Field agents are localized at ports and borders. They receive assignments from Team Leaders and physically manage the seal hardware. Below are the three core workflows for the field.

Tutorial 1: Journey Tagging (Start of Transit)

When a Journey Request enters the Tagging status, perform the lock installation:

  1. Open Request: Access the assigned Journey Request.
  2. Pre-Tagging Checks: Scroll to the Pre-Tagging section and complete the required Pre-Tagging Checklist (verifying battery levels, SIM card status, and mechanical integrity).
  3. Capture Evidence: Under the Documents & Photos section, upload your Pre-Tagging Pictures and Tagging Pictures (e.g., photo of the locked seal on the container).
  4. Confirm: Click the Confirm Tagging action button. The system will record the GPS location live from the seal and transition the journey to "Tagging In Progress".

Tutorial 2: Journey Untagging (End of Transit)

When a container arrives and the Control Room triggers a physical unlock, the Journey Request enters the Untagging status:

  1. Open Request: Access the assigned Journey Request.
  2. Physical Removal: Physically cut or remove the seal from the container doors.
  3. Capture Evidence: Navigate to the Untagging Documents & Photos section. Upload the required Entry Pictures and Untagging Pictures.
  4. Remarks: Record any tampering, damage, or observations in the Untagging Remarks field.
  5. Confirm: Click the Confirm Untagging action button to finalize the unlock event and initiate the return phase.

Tutorial 3: Seal Return (Custody Handoff)

Following an untagging (or after a remote unlock), the request enters the Awaiting Seal Return status:

  1. Transport: Transport the physical seal hardware back to the designated local custody point or warehouse.
  2. Open Request: Access the Journey Request one last time.
  3. Capture Evidence: Navigate to the Seal Return section and upload the final Seal Return Pictures showing the device being handed over.
  4. Confirm: Click the Confirm Seal Return action button. You will be prompted to confirm the return location, officially closing your assignment and returning the seal to the system's inventory pool.

Control Room Operator

Protocol for monitoring transits, approving journey setups, resolving exceptions, and handling tampering alarms.

Operations Room Workflows

Operators serve as the central nervous system for all transits, keeping constant track of active journeys via the Control Room Dashboard and acting as the final gatekeeper before a journey commences.

Tutorial 1: Journey Setup & Approvals

Before a container begins its transit, you must approve the hardware assignment:

  1. View Pending Requests: Navigate to the Journey Request List and filter for requests in the Pending Control Room Approval status.
  2. Hardware Check: Open a request. You can verify the assigned seal is responsive by clicking the Refresh Seal Status button. If the seal is defective, use the Swap Seal button to exchange it for a fresh unit.
  3. Approve: Provide any required Control Room Remarks and click the Approve button to authorize the Field Agent to begin tagging.

Tutorial 2: Alert Monitoring & Resolution

When a seal detects an anomaly (e.g., Seal Cut, Low Battery, Route Deviation), it generates an alert:

  1. Monitor Alerts: Open the Control Room tracking dashboard and switch to the Alerts tab (indicated by a bell icon).
  2. Acknowledge Alert: Locate the new alert row and click the Acknowledge button directly on the dashboard.
  3. Resolution Status: In the prompt, set the status to Underway (if you are investigating or dispatching response teams) or Resolved (if the issue is handled or confirmed as a false alarm).
  4. Remarks: Type in your resolution actions (e.g., "Contacted driver, legitimate detour") into the Remarks field and submit.

Tutorial: Unlocking a Seal (End Journey)

When a container reaches its destination, perform the unlock routine:

  1. Open Control Room: Navigate to the Control Room tracking dashboard.
  2. Select Journey: Search and locate the target active journey in the side panel.
  3. Trigger Unlock: Click the End Journey / Unlock button.
  4. Select Mode:
    • Physical Unlock: Creates an untagging assignment for a Field Tagging Agent.
    • Remote Unlock: Skips untagging and schedules a direct seal return assignment.
  5. Confirm: Accept the prompt. The final unlock location is captured live from the seal's GPS.

Finance Manager

Managing client contracts, seal billing rates, approving customer bookings, and reviewing/settling monthly statements.

Invoicing and Approvals

Ensuring all tracking operations are billed accurately according to contracts. The Finance Manager is responsible for authorizing jobs before field agents are deployed, setting up rate rules, and settling finalized trips.

Tutorial 1: Booking Approvals (Job Order Creation)

Before a tagging job can start, Finance must approve the customer booking:

  1. View Bookings: Open the Tagging Booking List and filter for requests in the Pending Finance PCB Approval status.
  2. Review Contract: Open the booking and ensure the customer's financial account is in good standing.
  3. Approve or Amend:
    • Click Actions > Approve to authorize the booking. This automatically generates a PCB Job Order for the operations team.
    • If the booking details need correction, click Actions > Reopen / Amend, provide a reason, and send it back to the Account Manager.

Tutorial 2: How to Set Billing for a Customer

Finance Managers apply custom rules to clients via the Current Customers module:

  1. Navigate: Open the Current Customers page.
  2. Open Modal: Find the target customer and click Set billing (or the existing rule label) in the Billing Type column.
  3. Choose Type: Select either Subscription or Leasing.
    • If Subscription: Pick a pre-configured Billing Rule from the dropdown. If the customer purchased their devices, check Outright Purchase and enter the Number of Seals Owned.
    • If Leasing: Proceed to the Rate Terms section to manually input the Currency, First Period Amount, First Period Days, and Extra Day Rate.
  4. Save: Verify the Tax category and click Save Billing to apply the rules.

Tutorial 3: Billing Follow-up & Settlement

Once a journey completes, Finance must review the generated charges and mark the journey as billed:

  1. View Pending Billing: Open the Billing Follow-up List to view all seal journeys currently in the Pending Billing status.
  2. Manage Billing: Click on a journey row to open its Manage Billing dashboard.
  3. Review Charges: Review the calculated totals, including the base First Period charges and any calculated Extra Days amounts.

System Administrator

Configuring API keys, user management, and setting up master checkpoints.

App Configuration

Maintains the foundational backend of the custom app:

  • API Settings: Map cellular telemetry parameters, set cron job intervals, and adjust SMS/Email notification templates.
  • Master Data: Manage checkpoints, vehicle lists, and device acquisitions.
